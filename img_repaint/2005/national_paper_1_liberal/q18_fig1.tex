% 四棱锥 P-ABCD：底面为直角梯形（AB//DC，角DAB 为直角），PA 垂直底面；
% M 为 PB 中点；PA、AM、AD、AC、AB 为虚线，其余为实线
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm,line width=.6pt, font=\normalsize, >=Stealth]
  \coordinate (P) at (0,5.2);
  \coordinate (A) at (0,2.0);
  \coordinate (B) at (5.6,2.0);
  \coordinate (D) at (-1.2,0.6);
  \coordinate (C) at (1.8,0.6);
  \coordinate (M) at (2.8,3.6);
  % 棱（实线）
  \draw (P) -- (D);
  \draw (P) -- (C);
  \draw (P) -- (B);
  \draw (D) -- (C);
  \draw (C) -- (B);
  \draw (M) -- (C);
  % 遮挡线（虚线）
  \draw[dashed] (P) -- (A);
  \draw[dashed] (A) -- (D);
  \draw[dashed] (A) -- (C);
  \draw[dashed] (A) -- (M);
  \draw[dashed] (A) -- (B);
  \node[above] at (P) {$P$};
  \node[left] at (A) {$A$};
  \node[right] at (B) {$B$};
  \node[below left] at (D) {$D$};
  \node[below] at (C) {$C$};
  \node[above right] at (M) {$M$};
\end{tikzpicture}

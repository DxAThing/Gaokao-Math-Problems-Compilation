\begin{problem}
已知集合 \( S_{m} = \{X \mid X = (x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{m}), x_{i} \in \{0, 1\}, i = 1, 2, \cdots, n\} \) (\( n \geqslant 2 \))，对于 \( A = (a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}) \)，\( B = (b_{1}, b_{2}, \cdots, b_{n}) \in S_{m} \)，定义 \( A \) 与 \( B \) 的差为 \( A - B = (|a_{1} - b_{1}|, |a_{2} - b_{2}|, \cdots, |a_{n} - b_{n}|) \); \( A \) 与 \( B \) 之间的距离为 \( d(A, B) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |a_{i} - b_{i}| \). (1) 证明: \(\forall A, B, C \in S_m\)，有 \(A - B \in S_m\)，且 \(d(A - C, B - C) = d(A, B)\); (2) 证明: \(\forall A, B, C \in S_m, d(A, B), d(A, C), d(B, C)\) 三个数中至少有一个是偶数. (3) 设 \(P \in S_m\)，\(P\) 中有 \(m (m \geqslant 2)\) 个元素，记 \(P\) 中所有两元素间距离的平均值为 \(\overline{d}(P)\). 证明: \(\overline{d}(P) \leqslant \frac{1}{2(m - 1)}\).
\end{problem}
